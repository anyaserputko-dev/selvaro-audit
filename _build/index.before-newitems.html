The short version

We built selvaro.de, so this is a look from the inside at what to build next.

What we noticed. On a phone a cookie window covers the whole screen, the Lernturm page loads an 18 MB animation, the buy button sits only at the top of a ten-screen page, 7 of 13 products look available but cannot be bought, and the cart does not show the way to free shipping.

What we propose. Ten changes, each on one screen — today on the left, after on the right.

Questions before we startThings we could not verify from outside, or that need your decision.

  • 01The seven products without stock. Is a return date known for the Wendehocker, Bücherregal and the five toys?
  • 02Payment methods. The cart shows Shop Pay, PayPal and Google Pay as express buttons; we could not open the checkout itself without placing an order.
  • 03Speed score. Google’s PageSpeed API refused every request today (daily quota).
  • 04Wishlist. The wishlist we built on Klaviyo is switched off in the theme (display:none).
  • 05Delivery time. 4–6 or 5–10 working days — which one should the whole site say?
  • 06Email domain. The domain’s SPF record does not list Shopify or Klaviyo and DMARC is set to “none”.

Next step

We agree on the five first-wave items and start there — the cookie window, the animation, the pinned buy button, the “Warteliste” badges and the free-shipping line in the cart. 16 hours of work, and it is the part that changes how many of today’s visitors reach the checkout.
